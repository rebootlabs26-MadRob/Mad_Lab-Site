*About the Mad Lab*

Where Tech Gets a Second Life

🔧 About ReBoot Lab — PCB | ER

ReBoot Lab was born from a simple mission: to give every device a fighting chance. Started 3+ years ago in a bedroom cluttered with cables and broken electronics, we've grown into a trusted name for custom PC builds and electronics repair.

What started as a hobby—fixing friends' computers, salvaging discarded hardware, and learning by trial and error—evolved into a passion. We've built hundreds of custom gaming rigs from entry-level to high-end. We've resurrected laptops, rescued data from failing drives, and optimized systems that seemed beyond hope. Every project is a puzzle, and we don't stop until it's solved.

Over the years, we've learned frameworks like systems, servers, networks, security testing, automation, and more. These skills combined with innovative diagnostics and cloud-free, local-first philosophies allow us to build customized solutions that work the way YOU need them to.

We're not a faceless corporate repair shop. We're tech enthusiasts who believe in transparency, fair pricing, and doing the job right the first time. When you bring your tech to ReBoot Lab, you're working with someone who genuinely cares about making your system perform better than ever.

🧪 The Mad Lab Philosophy

Technology should work for you—not the other way around. Whether it's a custom-built beast of a gaming rig or fixing an old laptop with sentimental value, we approach every project with the same level of care and craftsmanship.

We don't believe in planned obsolescence or throwing away perfectly good hardware. With the right upgrades and optimization, most systems can be brought back to peak performance. And when it's time for something new, we build systems designed for longevity with upgradeable components—maximizing your investment as technology evolves.
